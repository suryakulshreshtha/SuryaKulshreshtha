# How to Learn Programming & Software Development (Android & iOS)

## Start with Reading the Right Articles

Do not fall into clickbait and fancy headlines of videos/tutorials/articles.

## Programming Requires Two Things

Another important thing that mostly goes ignored is that programming—be it Android or iOS/Swift—requires you to learn:

1. **How to implement logic** and provide structure to support your needs (Design and Create)
2. **How to learn the tools/IDE/software seamlessly** so you can focus on doing and coding rather than struggling to set environment variables, checking installed software and directories.

## The Boost to Code Again

Understand we always prepare ourselves to learn the hard part by watching other successful people or reading or sometimes intuitively the boost to write code again surfaces.

**What initially stops you and gets you tired is the switch on and offs & set create or prepare the setup to go and actually sit and code.**

Two hours into your selected course of choice or maybe just following article step by step you realise this is just where i was able to press button and run the tools—actual coding begins now. That is at times tiring for beginner & expert alike.

## Your First Hello World

Everyone wants to type their first Hello World program (basically choose your weapon of choice and learn the respective syntax, print that on some fancy colored text editor or on actual cockpit styled IDE) & you have it—some corner of screen would say `Hello World`.

That's your first actual app in progress, but reaching this level would require you to do bunch of things first & that is tiring.

## Divide Your Learning Into Threshold & Liftoff

So divide your learning into a threshold & liftoff sequences.

**You won't find this perception in any book**—it is coming from personal but valid failure and initial struggle.

Every course will skip or be quick with this initial part of threshold. **Find a course or program or educator who helps you get on board easy and well first.**

Because everybody can teach you what book says—beyond that all programming concepts are same in beginning. Once you know that you can then specialize into Android specific aspects.

## Final Thought

All that i wrote above applies specifically to but also not limited to Android programming in Java / Kotlin / Flutter.

**Have fun & begin correctly.**
